    How the score is made: Mars-likeness (0–100) = 50% × coldness + 50% × dryness.
    coldness = clamp((25 − T) / 45, 0, 1)  ·  dryness = clamp(1 − R / 5, 0, 1)
    T = temperature at 2 m (°C), R = rain (mm/day). Data: NASA POWER, 2025.
